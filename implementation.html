How to Implement This at Your School

This guide is designed as a step-by-step playbook for schools, clubs, and student leaders. Follow each phase in order to launch, teach, and sustain the project.

Implementation Progress Tracker

Step 1

Assess Need

Collect baseline data and identify the school-specific challenge.

Step 2

Build Team

Recruit an advisor, student leaders, and support volunteers.

Step 3

Train Leaders

Teach the toolkit using presentations and role-based practice.

Step 4

Launch Pilot

Run a short pilot cycle and capture implementation notes.

Step 5

Measure Impact

Record participation, outcomes, and stakeholder feedback.

Step 6

Sustain Program

Transfer ownership to school leaders with clear documentation.

Detailed Guide

  1. Form a planning team and assign defined roles (lead, logistics, outreach, measurement).
  2. Run a needs assessment survey and review results with school partners.
  3. Customize materials for your campus context while preserving core outcomes.
  4. Train participants and document attendance, goals, and commitments.
  5. Execute a pilot event or learning cycle; gather immediate feedback.
  6. Analyze results and share a short impact report with stakeholders.
  7. Create a continuity plan that names next student leaders and advisor checkpoints.

Evidence Checklist for Councils and Schools

  • Needs assessment completed and summarized
  • Training materials delivered to peer leaders
  • National and/or global context clearly communicated
  • Impact metrics documented with before/after indicators
  • Leadership transition plan in place for sustainability

Adjust the steps to fit your school calendar and staffing model.
