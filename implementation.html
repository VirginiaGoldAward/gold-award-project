How to Implement This at Your School

This guide is designed as a step-by-step playbook for schools, clubs, and student leaders. Follow each phase in order to launch, teach, and sustain the project.

Implementation Guide

Step 1: Ask for Permission

Before you can even start thinking about building something like this in your community, it is very important that you ask for permission to make changes to the building. I worked with my AP’s and Principal to develop a plan that would best suit the needs of my school.

Step 2: Make a Plan

This is the step where you create a budget for your project, start thinking about what materials you would like to use, and who you want to help you with this project.

Step 3: Meet With Volunteers and Train Team Members

After deciding who you want to help you, you have to make sure they know how to help in the best way possible. Do they know how to use a power drill? Do they know the best type of caulk to use for installation? Do they know what to do if someone gets hurt? Teach your team about all these things and make sure you have the right people for the job. This is also where you want to work with getting help from a program in your community like National Beta Club who can help you keep your stations stocked, clean, and running! Don’t forget to reach out to the nurses office. Most schools in Georgia are part of a program called the RedDrop that provides free feminine products to all nurses offices. Nurses often have way more than they need and are usually willing to donate their excess materials to your cause.

Step 4: Gather Materials

This is where you get to go shopping! You need many things like a 3-tiered wire file holder, clear silicon based caulk, a power-drill, paper towels, drill bits, screws, and a level. You also need your feminine products. I would recommend working with your school nurse to see if they can help by donating their extras to your cause.

Step 5: Installation

This is where all your hard work and planning pay off! Get your team together and actually build and install the stations in your bathrooms!

Materials

List of materials and where to get them:

  • 3-tiered wire file holder (Amazon)
    • This one comes with a manual screw-driver, wall anchors, and screws to help with installation
  • Clear Silicon Based Caulk (Home Depot)
  • Caulk Gun (Home Depot)
  • Power Drill (Home Depot)
  • Drill Bits (Home Depot)
  • Level (Home Depot)
  • Pencils
  • Paper Towels

Installation Guide

How to Install:

  1. Clean wall where station is going to be placed
  2. Measure where the holes in the shelf is to determine where to drill
  3. Drill into the wall using a drill bit
  4. Place wall anchors in holes
  5. Build stations following given instructions
  6. Screw stations into wall with power drill ensuring station is level with a level
  7. Caulk perimeter of station to ensure stability and adhesion to the wall
  8. Once caulk is dry, add products as needed
