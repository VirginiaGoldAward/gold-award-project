My Experience

This page documents my leadership growth, team collaboration, and lessons learned while delivering a sustainable Gold Award project.

Leadership and Team Network

I led a cross-functional network that included educators, student volunteers, and community partners. I delegated responsibilities by strengths, established timelines, and ran regular progress check-ins.

My Role: Project lead, curriculum planner, outreach coordinator

Team Structure: Advisors, student ambassadors, implementation volunteers

Leadership Skills Built: Strategic planning, facilitation, impact reporting

Experience Timeline

  1. Discovery: Gathered data and clarified root causes.
  2. Design: Built project framework and teaching materials.
  3. Pilot: Tested activities and collected feedback.
  4. Refine: Improved toolkit based on response data.
  5. Scale: Prepared replication guide for other schools.

What I Learned

The most important lesson was that leadership is about creating systems other people can run. Sustainable impact comes from training others, documenting processes, and building momentum through shared ownership.

This project strengthened my confidence in public speaking, strategic communication, and long-term community engagement.
