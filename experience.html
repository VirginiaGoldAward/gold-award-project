My Experience

Through my project I learned and further developed many leadership skills. I learned how to work with and lead a team, how to better communicate in a formal manner, I improved my public speaking skills, and I got to give back to my community in a meaningful way!

Leadership and Team Network

I led a cross-functional network that included educators, student volunteers, and community partners. I delegated responsibilities by strengths, established timelines, and ran regular progress check-ins.

My Role: Project lead, curriculum planner, outreach coordinator

Team Structure: Advisors, student ambassadors, implementation volunteers

Leadership Skills Built: Strategic planning, facilitation, impact reporting, Public Speaking

SMART Goals

In order to receive this award, Girl Scouts must complete their predetermined SMART Goals which we set for ourselves before starting our project to track our growth. For this project I had 3 SMART Goals:

  1. Provide free, reliable access to essential feminine products in student bathrooms at my high school where 50% of products will be replaced each week.
  2. Have 15-20 people visit my website which teaches the community about my project and how to implement a similar movement in their own communities.
  3. Reach out to 5 other Girl Scouts and teach them how to initiate a program at their schools.
