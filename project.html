What Is a Gold Award Project?

The Girl Scout Gold Award is the highest honor in Girl Scouting. It requires identifying a root cause, designing a sustainable solution, leading a team, and creating measurable community impact. Projects must teach others, demonstrate leadership, and connect to broader issues beyond one local site.

Root Cause Analysis

My root cause analysis showed this issue was not caused by lack of care, but by gaps in awareness, process ownership, and accessible materials. I used interviews, a baseline survey, and educator feedback to identify key barriers and rank them by urgency and impact.

  1. Barrier 1: Stakeholders did not have a clear, shared process.
  2. Barrier 2: Existing resources were inconsistent and hard to use.
  3. Barrier 3: No student-led continuity model existed.

My final strategy directly addresses each barrier with structured training, standard resources, and a peer leadership model.
